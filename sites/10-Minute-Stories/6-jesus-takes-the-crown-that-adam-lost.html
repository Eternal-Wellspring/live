The Fall

Yahweh made a garden. He put a man and a woman in it. He spent time with them there, walking in the evening breeze. Then they listened to the serpent. They ate what He told them not to eat. And everything changed.They hid from Him. They were sent out of the garden, separated from Yahweh. The way to the tree of life was guarded. 1 And Yahweh said a hard thing to Adam. You came from dust, and you will go back to dust. 2 Death had come into the world. And it never left.
1 Gen 3:242 Gen 3:19

The Flood

People multiplied. But so did evil. Within a few generations it was everywhere. Yahweh looked at people and saw evil everywhere. Not just in what they did, but in what they thought. Every plan in every heart was evil, all the time. 1 Violence was everywhere. 2  It broke His heart. He was sorry He had ever made people.But one man was different. Yahweh was pleased with Noah. 3  Noah did what was right. 4  He lived close to God.So Yahweh told Noah to build an ark. Then the rain came. The whole world went under. Only Noah, his family and the animals with them survived.It was a fresh start. But the problem had survived too. It was inside the people on the ark.
1 Gen 6:52 Gen 6:113 Gen 6:84 Gen 6:9

Babel

After the flood, Yahweh gave them the same order He gave Adam: have children, spread out and fill the earth. 1 They multiplied. But they did not want to fill the earth. They wanted to stay together and build."Let's build a city," they said, "with a tower that reaches the sky. Let's make a name for ourselves, so we don't get scattered." 2 They wanted their own name, not His. They wanted to stay where they chose, not go where He sent them.So Yahweh came down. He confused their language. 3  They could no longer understand each other. And Yahweh scattered them across the whole earth. 4 That is how the nations began. Divided. Scattered. Turned away from the God who had made them.
1 Gen 9:12 Gen 11:43 Gen 11:74 Gen 11:9

The Nations and Their Gods

The nations did not stop worshipping. They just worshipped something else.Moses warned Israel never to worship the sun, the moon and the stars. Yahweh had handed those over to the other nations. 1  So the nations served gods they did not even know. 2 The Bible hints that these were not just statues. A psalm pictures God standing in a council of powerful spirit 'gods' and judging them. 3  In Daniel, an angel fights a spirit ruler called the prince of Persia. 4 The nations had their gods. Seen and unseen. And none of them was Yahweh.So what would He do with a world that had turned its back on Him?
1 Deu 4:192 Deu 29:24-263 Psa 82:14 Dan 10:13

Abraham

He started with one man.Yahweh told Abram to leave his country. He promised to turn him into a great nation. 1 The people at Babel tried to make a name for themselves. But Yahweh promised to make Abram's name great Himself. 2 And the promise was not just for Abram. Through him, every family on earth would be blessed. 3 All the families. Including the scattered nations.Yahweh changed his name to Abraham, which means father of many. Many nations would come from him. So would kings. 4 Paul says the promise was even bigger. Abraham would inherit the whole world. 5 One man. One promise. The whole world in view.
1 Gen 12:1-22 Gen 12:23 Gen 12:34 Gen 17:5-65 Rom 4:13

Yahweh's Own Nation

From Abraham came Isaac. From Isaac came Jacob. Jacob was renamed Israel. His sons became twelve tribes.The other nations had their gods. But Israel was different. Yahweh kept this nation for Himself. They were His own share of the world. 1 He rescued them from Egypt. He gave them His law. He lived among them in a special tent, the tabernacle.Yahweh said He had made this people for Himself. 2 Israel was meant to show the nations what Yahweh is like.They often failed. They chased the gods of the nations around them. But Yahweh did not give up on them.
1 Deu 32:92 Isa 43:21

The King

Out of Israel came a shepherd boy named David.He was the youngest son. Nobody expected much of him. But Yahweh does not look at the outside. He looks at what a person is like inside. 1 David became king. And Yahweh made him a promise. One of David's own descendants would rule after him, and Yahweh would make his kingdom last forever. 2 Forever is a long time. David died. His sons died. The kingdom fell apart.So who was this promise really about?
1 1Sa 16:72 2Sa 7:12-13

The Messiah

Hundreds of years later, a baby was born in David's town.Matthew starts his book by calling Jesus the son of David and the son of Abraham. 1 Son of Abraham. The blessing for all nations.Son of David. The King who reigns forever.Jesus grew up. He healed the sick. He forgave sinners. He taught about the kingdom of God.And through all of it, He never sinned. He was tempted in every way we are. 2  Yet He never once gave in. 3 Everyone else since Adam had fallen. Every single person has sinned. 4  He was the only man who never did.
1 Mat 1:12 Heb 4:153 1Pe 2:224 Rom 3:23

Death Could Not Hold Him

Jesus obeyed His Father all the way to the cross. He chose to suffer and die.They put His body in a tomb. They rolled a stone across it.But death only has a claim on those who sin. And He had never sinned.So God broke death's grip and raised Him up. Death could not keep hold of Him. 1 On the third day the tomb was empty.Forty days later, His followers watched Him rise into the sky until a cloud hid Him from view. 2 He went home to His Father.
1 Act 2:242 Act 1:9

All Authority

Where is Jesus now?God sat Him down at His own right hand in heaven. 1 
The place of highest honour and power. 2  Far above the rulers and gods of every nation. Jesus said it Himself; He has been given all authority, in heaven and on earth. 3 Angels, authorities and powers all answer to Him now. 4 When the nations were scattered at Babel, Yahweh handed them over to their gods. 5  Now there is a new King over all of them.
1 Rev 4:2-52 Eph 1:20-213 Mat 28:184 1Pe 3:225 Deu 32:8 ESV

Calling People in the Nations

So what did Jesus do with that authority?He sent His followers out to teach people in every nation to follow Him. 1 At Babel, languages split people apart. Then on the day of Pentecost, His followers spoke in their language, and people from all over the world 2 heard about Yahweh in their own language.The scattering was starting to reverse.But the job is not finished yet. Look around. Not everything obeys Him yet. 3  Jesus is waiting until every enemy lies at His feet. 4 
1 Mat 28:192 Act 2:53 Heb 2:84 Heb 10:12-13

Defeating the Rebellious Wicked

One day Jesus will come back. Not as a baby but as King. The whole world will see Him coming on the clouds, full of power and blazing glory. 1 Heaven opens. Jesus rides out on a white horse, a warrior King. 2  Heaven's armies ride with Him. 3 The dead and the living believers are swept up to greet Him. 4  In an instant, they are given bodies that never decay. They can never die. They can never sin. 5  They become just like Jesus. 6  Healed and whole. 7 They are holy, with no stain or flaw. 8  Not only forgiven, but good in whatever they do. 9 But the nations do not welcome Him. The beast and the world's kings gather their armies to fight Him. 10  It is Babel again. People united against Yahweh.It does not last. The beast is captured. 11  The serpent from the garden is chained up, so he can no longer deceive the nations. 12 Jesus sets His feet on the Mount of Olives. 13  The same hill He left from.Before He left, His followers had asked if He was about to give the kingdom back to Israel. 14  This is when He does just that!Yahweh gathers His scattered people. He brings Israel home from every country they were scattered to. 15  And He gives them the King He promised David. 16 The promise to David is kept. His Son sits on the throne. Forever.
1 Mat 24:302 Rev 19:113 Rev 19:144 1Th 4:16-175 1Co 15:52-536 1Jn 3:27 Isa 35:4-68 Eph 5:279 Rev 19:810 Rev 19:1911 Rev 19:2012 Rev 20:2-313 Zec 14:414 Act 1:615 Ezk 37:2116 Ezk 37:24

God's Kingdom comes on Earth

Then He will rule on the earth. His people rule with Him for a thousand years. 1 Israel was always meant to show the nations what Yahweh is like. Now it does. Ten people from every language grab one Jewish man by the sleeve. Take us with you, they say. We have heard that God is with you. 2 So the nations come to learn. They climb Yahweh's mountain and ask Him to teach them how to live. His teaching goes out from Jerusalem to the whole world. 3 So the nations learn a new way. They melt down their swords and spears and make farm tools out of them. No nation attacks another. Nobody even trains for war any more. 4 Think back. Not long after the garden, a man named Lamech bragged that he had killed a man just for wounding him. 5 By the days of Noah, evil filled every man. Every thought and plan was evil, all the time. 6 Now it is turned around. The nations learn peace instead of violence. Nobody hurts or destroys anything on Yahweh's holy mountain. The whole earth is full of people who know Yahweh, as full as the sea is of water. 7 Where people obey Yahweh, the land responds. He promised Israel that if they kept His commands, their land would give them big harvests. 8 As the nations obey, the earth is renewed. Harvests are so big that the ploughing starts again before the reaping is done. 9  Ruined land becomes like the garden of Eden. 10  The wolf and the lamb eat together. 11 Yahweh is King over the whole earth. 12  What began at Babel is undone.And the promise to Abraham is kept. Yahweh chose one man. From that one man came one nation on the earth.Through the Messiah, Abraham's family has spread across the whole world. Everyone who belongs to Christ counts as Abraham's family and shares in what was promised. 13 Yahweh's promise to Abraham comes true. Through his family line, every nation is blessed. 14  One man. One nation. Now a family in every nation. Abraham inherits the world, just as Paul said. 15 He rules until every enemy is beaten. The last enemy to go is death. 16  The enemy that came in at the garden is gone for good.Then comes the end. He hands the kingdom over to God the Father, 17 so that God is everything to everyone. 18  The garden lost. The world flooded. The nations scattered. And at the end, every nation home with Yahweh.

Yahweh Dwells With Mankind

Then something happens that has not happened since the garden.John saw it in a vision. The old heaven and earth were gone. In their place stood a new heaven and a new earth. 1 The holy city, New Jerusalem, comes down out of heaven from God. 2  And a loud voice from heaven announces the news: God now lives with people. They are His people, and God Himself is with them as their God. 3 Yahweh promised this long ago. Through Moses He said He would walk among His people and be their God. 4  Through Ezekiel He said He would make His home with them. 5 Through Zechariah He told Jerusalem to sing for joy, because He was coming to live among them. And many nations would join Yahweh and become His people too. 6 God wipes every tear from their eyes. No more death. No more grief. No more crying. 7 Nothing is under a curse any more. 8  His people see His face. 9 At the start, the garden was lost. A flaming sword was placed to guard the way to the tree of life. 10 Now the garden is back. And this time it covers the whole earth.John saw a river of life-giving water, clear as glass, flowing from the throne of God and of Jesus. 11  The tree of life grows on both banks. Its leaves heal the nations. 12 No sword. No guard. Jesus promised that everyone who wins through will eat from the tree of life in God's paradise. 13 Paradise, open to every nation. And Yahweh with His people. Forever.
1 Rev 21:12 Rev 21:23 Rev 21:34 Lev 26:125 Ezk 37:276 Zec 2:10-117 Rev 21:48 Rev 22:39 Rev 22:410 Gen 3:2411 Rev 22:112 Rev 22:213 Rev 2:7

Where Do You Fit?

That is the story of the world, from the fall to the kingdom.It is not just about nations. It is about people. People like you.Every nation is made of people who choose. Either their own name, like Babel. Or Yahweh's like Abraham.If you say out loud that Jesus is Lord, and truly believe that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved. 1 The King has all authority. He is gathering His people from every nation.Will you be one of them?
1 Rom 10:9
