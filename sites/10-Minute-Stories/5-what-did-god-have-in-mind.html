1. Paradise of God: Restored
God dwelt with Mankind until they rebelled. God will return so that He may once again dwell in Paradise with us.
5. What did God have in mind?
God's desire for a family. Appreciate the obstacles in His path and His strategy to have His way.

The Goal

Before there was a sun, a star or a single grain of sand, there was God. And God had a plan.He did not need anything. He was not lonely or bored. But He wanted something, and He wanted it with all His heart. He wanted a family: people who would know Him, love Him and belong with Him for ever.Not servants kept at a distance. Not robots who had no choice. A family, at home with Him.So, before the foundation of the world, He chose them. Before anyone had done anything good or bad, He had already decided who He would make, and what He would make them for.That is where this story really begins. Not with a garden, or a flood, or a king, but with a heart that wanted us.

The Problems

God knew that a real family comes with real risks.Love has to be free. If you cannot say no, your yes means nothing. So the people He made would need the freedom to turn away. And freedom can go wrong.He knew there would be evil: a rebel who would whisper lies and pull others away from God.He knew wrong choices would bring sin and sin is baggage. Some of it was handed to you before you could walk. Some you picked up yourself. Some you let others dump on you. Any baggage  pulls you further from God.

God wants to be with you. But your baggage comes between you and Him. The farther you walk from God, the wider it gets. No one jumps that gap. No ladder reaches. The only way across is a bridge, and we can’t build it.He knew wrong choices would bring sin, and that sin always ends in death.
He knew the world would fill with injustice: the strong crushing the weak, and the guilty walking free.And worst of all, He knew the friendship at the centre of it all would break.Any one of these could have ended the plan. God saw them all, and went ahead anyway.So He made the world, and it was good. He made man in His own image, male and female, to carry His likeness like children carry their father's face. That is who we are. That is where we belong.And He came down to be with them. Picture it: a garden in the evening, and the sound of the Lord God walking in the garden in the cool of the day. Not a distant ruler. A Father who came looking for His children's company.Then it happened, just as He knew it could. They listened to the lie. They chose their own way. And the next time God came walking, they hid among the trees.The friendship was broken. Death came into the world. The family was locked out of home.

The Promise

Here is the remarkable thing. God did not give up, and He did not start again with someone else.Right there, in that broken garden, He made a promise. A child would one day be born, and God told the serpent, He shall bruise your head. Evil would not have the last word.The rest of the Old Testament is God keeping that promise alive. Through families and nations, through kings who failed and prophets who wept, He kept saying the same thing: I am still here. I have not forgotten. Someone is coming.God is in charge, and He is committed. Nothing people did could stop Him, and nothing could make Him walk away.

The Way Back

But how could a broken family come home? Sin is real. Death is real. You cannot just pretend they are not there.God's answer had been ready all along. Scripture calls Him the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world. The rescue was planned before the problem ever began.God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son. The Messiah came, God's own Son, born as a baby, and grew up among us. He healed the sick, welcomed outsiders and told the truth.Then, on a hill outside Jerusalem, He was nailed to a cross. He did nothing wrong, yet He took the full weight of everything we had done. He was wounded for our transgressions. Our sin, our death, our broken friendship with God, all of it fell on Him.Three days later, some women came to His grave, expecting a body. The stone was rolled away. The tomb was empty. He is not here; for He is risen.Death had met someone it could not hold.So when people ask how to get back to God, the answer is not a list of rules. It is a person. Jesus said, I am the way, the truth, and the life. He is the door back into the family. Anyone who trusts Him can come home.

Putting Things Right

But look around. The world is still full of cruelty and lies. Have you ever wondered if wrong will ever really be put right?God has. He cares about justice far more than we do. And He has fixed the day. He will judge the world in righteousness by the Man He has chosen, and He proved it by raising Him from the dead.Things will get darker before they get better. There will be a time of great trouble on the earth. But it will not last.Picture this. The sky opens, and there is a white horse. The One riding it is called Faithful and True. The same Jesus who once came quietly as a baby now comes back as King, openly, for the whole world to see.He gathers His people. He deals with evil once and for all. And He reigns over the earth with fairness, so that the weak are protected, the truth is honoured, and no one gets away with wrong any more.Justice comes first. Only a world that has been put right can be made perfect.

The Finish

And then, at last, the goal.Earth is made new. Not floated away somewhere, but renewed, healed and whole. God Himself says, Behold, I make all things new.And the thing He wanted before time began finally happens. A loud voice from heaven announces it: the tabernacle of God is with men. God is not visiting any more. He has moved in. He lives with His people, and they are His family for ever.Picture His hand on a tear-stained face. God will wipe away every tear. No more death. No more sorrow. No more crying or pain. All the problems He faced before the world began have been beaten, every single one.Freedom did not destroy His family. Evil did not win. Sin and death are gone. Every wrong has been put right. And the broken friendship is mended, face to face.It is the garden again, only better. And this time it is for ever.

Where Do You Fit?

That is the whole Bible in one story: God wanted a family, faced every obstacle, and won.Which means the question was never really whether God's plan would work. It will. The question is whether you will be part of it.He made you in His image. He wants you home. The way is open.
