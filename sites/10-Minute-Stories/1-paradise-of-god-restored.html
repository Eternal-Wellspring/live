1. Paradise of God: Restored
God dwelt with Mankind until they rebelled. God will return so that He may once again dwell in Paradise with us.

Introduction

What this story is about

​1. God created a very good world where mankind lived with Him.
2. Mankind rebelled and the mankind was separated from God.
3. God will return and live with mankind once more.The story actually begins before the world even existed.

1. Paradise

Before creation

Before God made anything, He already had a purpose. He wanted to live among His people and be their God. 2 God also knew that people would sin, and that their sin would separate them from Him. 3 So, before He made the world, God planned a way to bring them back to Himself: a perfect, spotless sacrifice to deal with their sin. The apostle Peter explains that God chose Christ for this sacrifice before the world was made, and that Christ was revealed in these last times, the final age that began when Christ came. 4 
2 Eph 1:4-5 Lev 26:123 Isa 59:24 1Pe 1:19-20

God's Word creates

Then God started by creating. God spoke, and light came into being. 1  God planted a garden in a place called Eden, put the first man, Adam, there, and set the tree of life in the middle of the garden. 2  Whoever ate from the tree of life would live forever. 3  God gave Adam and his wife, Eve, rule over the earth and told them to have children and fill the earth. God looked at everything He had made and saw that it was very good. 4  People lived with God, and the world was whole.
1 Gen 1:32 Gen 2:8-93 Gen 3:224 Gen 1:28-31

2. The Fall

Adam and Eve disobey

God gave Adam one rule. Adam could eat from any tree in the garden except the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, and God warned that if Adam ate from that tree he would surely die. 1  Adam and Eve ate from that tree, and then they hid from God, whose personal name is Yahweh. 2  Yahweh God sent Adam out of the garden, away from the tree of life. 3  The ground was cursed and grew thorns and thistles, and people would have to work hard for their food until they died and their bodies returned to the dust. 4  This is the break that the rest of the story sets out to repair. People were shut out from God's presence, and death had entered the world.
1 Gen 2:172 Gen 3:6-83 Gen 3:23-244 Gen 3:17-19

Mankind keeps rebelling

The break spread. Adam's son Cain murdered his brother Abel. 1  Heavenly beings called the sons of God took wives from among women on earth, 2 and the earth was filled with violence. 3  Yahweh sent a great flood on the earth, though He saved Noah and his family. 4  After the Flood, people at a place called Babel built a tower to make a name for themselves, so that they would not be scattered over the earth. 5  Yahweh scattered them across the whole earth anyway. 6 
1 Gen 4:82 Gen 6:23 Gen 6:114 Gen 7:1-45 Gen 11:46 Gen 11:8

A nation, and a nation exiled

God then made a fresh start with one family. Yahweh chose the descendants of a man named Abraham to be His own people, the nation of Israel. 1  Yahweh came down on Mount Sinai in fire and met with Israel there. 2  Yahweh told Israel to build a tabernacle, a large tent, so that He could live among them. 3  Yahweh's presence stayed in the innermost room, behind a veil. 4  Only the high priest could go behind the veil, only once a year, and never without the blood of a sacrifice, an animal killed to cover the people's sins. 5  Later a permanent temple in Jerusalem took the place of the tent. So God lived among His people again, but sin still kept them at a distance. Israel kept turning to idols. 6  The glory of Yahweh, the visible sign of His presence, left the temple, 7 and the people were taken away into exile in Babylon. 8 
1 Deu 7:62 Exo 19:183 Exo 25:84 Lev 16:25 Heb 9:76 Ezk 8:107 Ezk 10:188 2Ki 25:11

3. Paradise Restored

The promised Seed comes

Then the repair began. In Eden, God had promised that a Seed, a descendant of the woman, would crush the serpent. 1  At the time God had set, the promised Seed was born of a woman. 2  The promised Seed was Jesus, the Son of God, who became a human being and lived among people. 3  Jesus faced every kind of temptation people face, yet Jesus never sinned. 4  Passover was the night in Egypt when the blood of a lamb protected Israel's homes from death. Jesus died as the Passover lamb, the sacrifice God had planned before creation. 5  Jesus rose from the dead as the firstfruits, the first of everyone God will raise. 6  When Jesus died, the veil in the temple, the curtain that kept people out of God's presence, was torn in two. 7  The way back to God was open.
1 Gen 3:152 Gal 4:43 Jhn 1:144 Heb 4:155 1Co 5:76 1Co 15:207 Mat 27:51

The King returns

The story is not finished yet. The prophet Zechariah saw Yahweh coming with all His holy ones. 1  Revelation shows Christ, the King God promised, coming to judge and fight with justice. 2  Christ will bind the serpent of old, the Devil, 3 and God will bring the people of Israel back into their own land. 4  Christ will reign for a thousand years, 5 the nations will learn Yahweh's ways, 6 and the whole earth will know Yahweh as fully as water fills the sea. 7 
1 Zec 14:52 Rev 19:113 Rev 20:24 Ezk 37:215 Rev 20:46 Isa 2:37 Isa 11:9

God lives with His people

When every enemy, including death, has been put under Christ's feet, Christ hands the kingdom to God the Father. 1  A new city, the New Jerusalem, comes down out of heaven to a renewed earth, and God makes His home with people. 2  The curse that began in Eden is gone, God's servants see God's face, and the tree of life stands again. 3  The serpent is gone for good. 4  What God purposed before creation has come true: God lives among His people as their God.
1 1Co 15:24-262 Rev 21:1-33 Rev 22:2-44 Rev 20:10
