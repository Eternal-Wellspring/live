In the Beginning Yahweh wanted a Family to love

Before everything existed, Yahweh wanted to dwell with a human family in the garden of Eden. Love would the only language spoken in His presence. 1 God, who's name is Yahweh, planned that Eden would be filled with human beings and cover the whole earth. The Paradise of God where heaven and earth were together, forever with the Tree of Life. 2 
1Eph 1:3-62Rev 2:7
However, there is something missed out of that plan - the heavenly host were watching the creation of the earth and rejoicing - they are the sons of God. 3 They are spiritual beings who exist in the heavenly places with God and where God is, they will be there too.
3Job 38:4-7
As Yahweh finished creating the earth, His heavenly family rejoiced in heaven. Then He planted a garden for Adam and Eve, called Eden, the Paradise of God, where they walked with Him. There was the tree of life in the midst of the garden and everything was very good.
Yahweh had chosen to start with one couple, and all their descendants would fill the earth - every one of them created in His image. Yahweh gave each one the opportunity to serve as His ambassador, tending and keeping His garden.
