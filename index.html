Eternal Wellspring

The River of Life — God dwelling with His people

Jesus is the Way, the Truth and the Life

Eternal is the end God has always intended: He dwelling with His people, His life among us, without end. Wellspring is the River of Life — living water from His throne, not the wells this age keeps digging.

This world is full of fake news, deception, hatred, division, violence, tribalism, injustice, fear and intimidation. Those things wound nations and households. They cannot heal them.

The Word can. These expositions open God’s ways, His truth and His life in the Scriptures — practical for today, prophetic for the hope set before us, when Jesus the Son of God returns to earth. That is the answer to mankind’s woes: not another tribe, not another rumour, but the Way, the Truth and the Life.
