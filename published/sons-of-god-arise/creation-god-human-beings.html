In the Beginning Yahweh wanted a Family to love

Before everything existed, Yahweh planned to be with a human family forever. He already had a heavenly family and wanted a place where both could live with Him. Love would the only language that would be spoken.  1Jhn 3:16-17So He decided to make a planet, especially for humans. He would surround them with myriads of galaxies so they could look up and see His power and glory.He would fill the planet with all kinds of animals for the sky, land and water, with seasons to grow beautiful plants and tasty food.His human family would be His ambassadors on earth — reigning in His name and according to His Word.After ushering in God's Kingdom, with the help of His heavenly family, Earth would become the Paradise of God where heaven and earth come together.
Photo
As Yahweh finished creating the earth, His heavenly family rejoiced in heaven. Then He planted a garden for Adam and Eve, called Eden, the Paradise of God, where they walked with Him. There was the tree of life in the midst of the garden and everything was very good.
Yahweh had chosen to start with one couple, and all their descendants would fill the earth - every one of them created in His image. Yahweh gave each one the opportunity to serve as His ambassador, tending and keeping His garden.
